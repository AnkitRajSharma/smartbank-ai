# Digital Payments, Cards, and Net Banking

## Electronic Fund Transfer Channels
- **UPI (Unified Payments Interface)**:
  - Real-time 24x7 payment system linking bank accounts to a Virtual Payment Address (VPA) / UPI ID or mobile number.
  - Limits: Standard peer-to-peer (P2P) limit of ₹1,00,000 per day; up to ₹5,00,000 for hospital, educational, and tax payments.
  - Zero transaction charges for retail customers and standard merchant payments.
  - Security: Two-factor authentication requiring UPI PIN on customer's registered mobile device.
- **IMPS (Immediate Payment Service)**:
  - Instant interbank electronic fund transfer accessible 24x7x365 through internet banking and mobile apps.
  - Maximum limit: ₹5,00,000 per transaction. Immediate credit to beneficiary account.
- **NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer)**:
  - Operates 24x7 in half-hourly settlement batches.
  - No minimum or maximum transaction limit. Best for routine bill payments and transfers.
  - Zero charges for online NEFT initiated through internet banking or mobile apps.
- **RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement)**:
  - Real-time gross settlement of funds without batch waiting.
  - Minimum transaction amount: ₹2,00,000; no upper ceiling. Operates 24x7 for high-value fund transfers.

## Debit and Credit Cards
- **Debit Cards**:
  - EMV chip and PIN enabled for fraud protection.
  - Contactless / NFC Tap-and-Pay: Transactions up to ₹5,000 without requiring PIN entry.
  - Daily ATM withdrawal limit: ₹40,000 (SmartSaver) up to ₹1,00,000 (SmartWealth).
  - Online and international transactions can be switched ON/OFF instantly via mobile banking app.
- **Credit Cards**:
  - Interest-Free Period: 45 to 50 days from the start of the billing cycle (valid only if previous month balance is paid in full).
  - Minimum Amount Due (MAD): 5% of total outstanding balance. Paying only the MAD avoids late payment fees but attracts revolving interest (APR 36% to 42% p.a.) on the remaining balance from the date of purchase.
  - Cash Advance: Withdrawing cash on credit cards attracts immediate finance charges plus cash advance fee (no grace period).
  - Card Blocking / Hotlisting: Can be done instantly via mobile app, SMS to 56767, or 24/7 toll-free IVR.
