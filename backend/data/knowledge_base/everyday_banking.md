# Everyday banking safety

Use the bank's official app or the number printed on a payment card to report a lost card. Never share a PIN, password, one-time password (OTP), or full card number in chat. A bank will not ask a customer to install remote-access software or share an OTP to receive a refund. Verify unexpected payment requests through a separate trusted channel.

Savings account fees, interest, withdrawal limits, and eligibility vary by institution and product. Review the terms provided by the relevant bank. This synthetic SmartBank demonstration is not connected to a real bank and cannot make payments or transfers.
