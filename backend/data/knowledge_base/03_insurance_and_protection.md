# Insurance and Protection Products

## Term Life Insurance
- **SmartProtect Pure Term Plan**:
  - Financial safety net providing high life cover at low premiums.
  - Recommended sum assured: 10x to 20x annual gross income plus existing debt obligations.
  - Policy term: Up to 40 years or up to age 85 (whole life options up to age 99).
  - Payout options: Lump-sum payout, monthly income payout, or combination.
  - Optional Riders:
    - Comprehensive Critical Illness Rider: Lump sum payout on diagnosis of 36 critical illnesses (cancer, heart attack, kidney failure, stroke).
    - Accidental Death and Disability Rider: Additional sum assured in case of accidental demise or permanent total disability.
    - Waiver of Premium Rider: Future premiums waived upon permanent disability or critical illness diagnosis.
  - Tax Benefits: Premiums eligible for tax deduction under Section 80C up to ₹1,50,000; claim payouts exempt under Section 10(10D).

## Health Insurance (Mediclaim)
- **SmartHealth Shield Comprehensive Plan**:
  - Sum Insured options: ₹5 Lakhs, ₹10 Lakhs, ₹25 Lakhs, ₹50 Lakhs, ₹1 Crore, or Super Top-up plans.
  - Cashless hospitalization across 10,000+ network hospitals nationwide.
  - Inclusions: In-patient care, pre-hospitalization (60 days) and post-hospitalization (180 days), day-care procedures, modern robotic treatments, organ donor expenses, ambulance cover.
  - No Room Rent Capping (available on ₹10L+ sum insured).
  - No Claim Bonus (NCB): 10% to 50% cumulative increase in sum insured for every claim-free year, up to 100%.
  - Waiting Periods: 30 days initial waiting period (except accidents), 24 months for specified ailments (cataracts, hernia, joint replacement), 36 months for pre-existing diseases (PED).
  - Tax Benefits: Deductions under Section 80D up to ₹25,000 for self/family, and additional ₹50,000 for senior citizen parents (total deduction up to ₹75,000 to ₹1,00,000).

## Motor and General Insurance
- **Motor Insurance**:
  - Third-Party Liability: Mandatory by law under the Motor Vehicles Act; covers bodily injury, death, and property damage to third parties.
  - Comprehensive Car Insurance: Covers own vehicle damages (accidents, fire, natural disasters, theft) plus third-party liabilities.
  - Add-on Covers: Zero Depreciation (Bumper-to-Bumper), Engine & Gearbox Protection, Return to Invoice (RTI), Roadside Assistance (RSA), Consumables Cover.
- **Home and Property Insurance**:
  - Covers building structure and contents (furniture, electronics, appliances) against fire, lightning, earthquake, storm, flood, and burglary.

## Insurance Claim Settlement Guidelines
- Hospitalization Claims: For planned admission, notify TPA desk 48 hours prior; for emergency admission, notify within 24 hours. Submit discharge summary, doctor's prescription, pharmacy bills, and claim form.
- Life Claims: Intimation within 90 days with death certificate, original policy bond, medical reports, and claim form. Uncontested claims settled within 15-30 days as per IRDAI guidelines.
