# Credit Scores, Lending Guidelines, and Loans

## Credit Bureau Scores & Risk Classification
- Credit scores evaluate creditworthiness based on repayment history, credit utilization, credit age, credit mix, and recent inquiries (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF High Mark).
- **Score Brackets**:
  - **750 to 900 (Excellent / Prime)**: Lowest interest rates, waived processing fees, pre-approved loan offers, instant sanctioning.
  - **700 to 749 (Good)**: Favorable interest rates, standard processing, high probability of approval.
  - **650 to 699 (Fair / Moderate Risk)**: Additional documentation, lower loan-to-value (LTV), higher interest rates, or co-applicant requirement.
  - **300 to 649 (Poor / High Risk)**: High probability of rejection. Recommended to build credit history through secured credit cards or resolving past defaults before applying.
- **Credit Score Improvement Tips**: Keep credit utilization ratio under 30%, ensure zero late payments, avoid applying for multiple loans simultaneously, maintain a healthy mix of secured and unsecured debt, monitor credit report for errors.

## Debt-to-Income (DTI) & Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR)
- Lenders calculate the customer's total existing monthly EMI obligations divided by net monthly income.
- **Ideal FOIR**: Less than 40%.
- **Maximum Acceptable FOIR**: 50% for personal loans; up to 60% for high-net-worth home loan applicants.
- If existing EMIs exceed 50% of income, requested loan amounts are scaled down or rejected.

## Retail Loan Products
- **Personal Loans (Unsecured)**:
  - Loan amount: ₹50,000 to ₹40,00,000.
  - Tenure: 12 months to 60 months.
  - Interest rate: 10.49% to 18.50% p.a. (reducing balance).
  - Processing fee: 1.0% to 2.5% of loan amount.
  - Prepayment / Foreclosure: Allowed after 6 months; zero charges for individual floating rate loans.
- **Home Loans (Secured)**:
  - Loan amount: Up to ₹10 Crore (up to 80-90% of property cost depending on valuation).
  - Tenure: Up to 30 years.
  - Interest rate: Starting at 8.35% p.a. (floating, linked to external benchmark repo rate).
  - Tax Benefits: Deductions under Section 24(b) for interest up to ₹2,00,000 p.a. and Section 80C for principal repayment up to ₹1,50,000 p.a.
- **Auto / Car Loans**:
  - New Cars: Up to 90% of on-road price, tenure up to 7 years, interest rates 8.65% to 10.50% p.a.
  - Used Cars: Up to 80% of valuation, tenure up to 5 years, interest rates 11.00% to 14.50% p.a.
- **Education Loans**:
  - Domestic studies up to ₹20,00,000; Overseas studies up to ₹1.5 Crore. Moratorium period: Course duration + 6 to 12 months. Tax benefit under Section 80E on total interest paid.

## EMI Calculation Formula
- The Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI) on a reducing balance basis is calculated as:
  $$EMI = \frac{P \times r \times (1+r)^n}{(1+r)^n - 1}$$
  where $P$ is principal, $r$ is monthly interest rate ($\text{annual rate} / 12 / 100$), and $n$ is tenure in months.

## Required Documentation for Loans
- Identity Proof: PAN Card (mandatory), Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID.
- Address Proof: Aadhaar, Passport, Utility bill (under 2 months old), or Rental Agreement.
- Income Proof (Salaried): Latest 3 months salary slips, 6 months bank statements reflecting salary credits, Form 16 / ITR for last 2 years.
- Income Proof (Self-Employed): 2 years audited Balance Sheet & P&L statements, 2 years ITR with computation of income, 12 months business bank statements, GST registration and returns.
