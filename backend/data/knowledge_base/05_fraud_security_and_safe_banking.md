# Fraud Security, Cybercrime Prevention, and Safe Banking

## BFSI Information Security Directives
- **Zero-Tolerance Security Mandate**:
  - NEVER share your One-Time Password (OTP), UPI PIN, ATM PIN, Net Banking Password, CVV, or Card Expiry Date with anyone.
  - SmartBank AI and bank officials will NEVER ask you for your passwords, OTPs, PINs, or remote screen access under any circumstance.
  - Banks never request customers to transfer money to "safety accounts" or "verification wallets".

## Common BFSI Fraud Modalities & Countermeasures
- **Phishing & Smishing (SMS Phishing)**:
  - Fraudsters send SMS messages claiming "Your bank account / SIM card / electricity will be blocked, click here to update KYC".
  - Always verify official URLs (ensure domain matches the official bank website and starts with `https://`). Do not tap unverified links.
- **Vishing (Voice Call Scams)**:
  - Scammers pose as bank representatives offering credit card limit enhancement, loan approvals, or refund processing.
  - They ask the customer to read out an OTP sent to their phone. Reading out an OTP authorizes an unauthorized debit from your account.
- **Remote Access Application Scams**:
  - Fraudsters urge victims to install apps like AnyDesk, TeamViewer, QuickSupport, or RustDesk under the pretext of technical support.
  - Installing these gives criminals complete remote control of your phone and screen, allowing them to intercept OTPs and steal funds.
- **Fake QR Code & Collect Request Fraud**:
  - Fraudulent sellers or buyers on classifieds tell victims: "Scan this QR code or accept this UPI request to receive payment".
  - Golden Rule: Entering a UPI PIN ALWAYS DEBITS money from your account. You NEVER need to enter a UPI PIN or scan a QR code to RECEIVE money.
- **SIM Swap Fraud**:
  - If your mobile phone network suddenly disconnects or shows "No Service" for prolonged periods without network outage, contact your telecom provider immediately to check for fraudulent SIM replacement.

## SmartBank AI Automated Fraud Detection
- SmartBank AI evaluates transaction risk using anomaly detection machine learning models.
- Key Risk Signals:
  - High transaction amount relative to average transaction history.
  - Transactions conducted during unusual night hours (e.g., 1 AM to 4 AM).
  - Rapid succession of transactions within 24 hours (velocity spikes).
  - Significant geographic distance (>100 km) from typical customer transaction cluster.
  - International transaction flagged on a domestic profile.
- A high risk score triggers automated step-up authentication (SMS/App approval) and alerts the risk monitoring cell.

## Customer Action in Case of Fraud
- **Instant Hotlisting**: Block cards immediately via the SmartBank mobile app or call the 24/7 emergency toll-free helpline (1800-SMARTBANK).
- **RBI Limited Liability Policy**:
  - If reported within 3 days of unauthorized debit, the customer has ZERO liability.
  - If reported within 4 to 7 days, customer liability is capped (e.g., up to ₹10,000 for savings accounts).
  - If reported after 7 days, liability depends on bank policy.
- Report cyber fraud incidents to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) or helpline 1930.
