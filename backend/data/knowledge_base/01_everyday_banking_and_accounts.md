# Everyday Banking, Accounts, and Deposits

## Savings Accounts
- **SmartSaver Account**: Standard savings account offering 4.0% p.a. for balances up to ₹1,00,000 and 6.5% p.a. for balances above ₹1,00,000. Interest is calculated on daily closing balance and credited quarterly. Zero minimum balance requirement for salaried accounts; ₹2,000 average monthly balance for regular savings.
- **SmartWealth Privilege Account**: Premium account with relationship manager, complimentary airport lounge access, higher daily ATM cash withdrawal limit of ₹1,00,000 and daily POS limit of ₹3,00,000. Requires ₹1,00,000 minimum balance or ₹5,00,000 total relationship value.

## Current Accounts
- **SmartCurrent Business Account**: Designed for merchants, SMEs, and professionals. Free cash deposits up to ₹5,00,000 per month, unlimited free NEFT and RTGS transactions, 100 free cheque leaves per month. Minimum Average Monthly Balance (MAMB) is ₹10,000. Overdraft facility available against fixed deposits or business turnover.

## Fixed Deposits (FD) & Term Deposits
- **Interest Rates**:
  - 7 days to 45 days: 4.50% p.a.
  - 46 days to 179 days: 5.75% p.a.
  - 180 days to 364 days: 6.50% p.a.
  - 1 year to 2 years: 7.25% p.a.
  - Above 2 years to 5 years: 7.40% p.a.
  - 5 years to 10 years: 7.10% p.a.
  - Senior Citizens (Age 60+) receive an additional 0.50% p.a. across all tenures (up to 7.90% p.a.).
- **Compounding**: Interest is compounded quarterly. Re-investment (cumulative) or periodic payout (monthly/quarterly) options available.
- **Premature Withdrawal**: Allowed for all deposits except Tax-Saver FDs. A penalty of 0.50% to 1.00% is levied on the applicable rate for the duration the deposit was held.
- **Tax Saver FD**: 5-year fixed tenure eligible for tax deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act up to ₹1,50,000 per financial year. Premature withdrawal or loan against deposit is not permitted.

## Recurring Deposits (RD)
- Minimum monthly deposit: ₹500 (in multiples of ₹100).
- Tenures from 6 months up to 120 months.
- Rates match the corresponding Fixed Deposit tenure interest rates. Interest is compounded quarterly.
