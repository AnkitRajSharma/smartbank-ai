# Credit and loans

A credit score is a summary indicator based on a credit bureau's records and scoring method. The synthetic score shown by this local demonstration is illustrative only and is not a bureau score or a lending decision. Contact the relevant credit bureau or lender to review actual records.

Before borrowing, compare the annual interest rate, fees, repayment schedule, penalties, and total cost. Lenders may ask for identity, address, income, employment, and bank documentation. Requirements vary. A model estimate is not loan approval; only a lender can make a decision after its review.
