# Wealth Management, Investments, and Financial Planning

## Core Principles of Financial Health
- **50/30/20 Budgeting Allocation**:
  - 50% for Needs (Rent, EMIs, utilities, groceries, medical insurance).
  - 30% for Wants (Dining, entertainment, travel, lifestyle).
  - 20% for Savings and Investments (Emergency fund, retirement, mutual funds).
- **Emergency Reserve Fund**:
  - Maintain liquid reserves equal to 3 to 6 months of mandatory household expenses.
  - Park funds in high-liquidity instruments: SmartSaver savings account with auto-sweep or overnight/liquid debt mutual funds.
- **Debt Optimization**:
  - Prioritize paying off high-cost debt (credit cards at 36-42% APR and unsecured personal loans) before long-term investing.
  - Employ either the Avalanche Method (pay highest interest debt first) or Snowball Method (pay smallest balances first for psychological wins).

## Investment Instruments & Products
- **Systematic Investment Plans (SIP)**:
  - Rupee cost averaging into diversified mutual funds (Large Cap, Flexi Cap, Mid Cap).
  - Power of compounding: A monthly SIP of ₹10,000 for 15 years at an estimated 12% annual return compounds to approximately ₹50 Lakhs.
- **Public Provident Fund (PPF)**:
  - Sovereign-guaranteed long-term savings scheme with a 15-year maturity.
  - Enjoys EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt) tax status under Section 80C. Annual investment limit ₹1,50,000.
- **Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB)**:
  - Government securities denominated in grams of gold.
  - Earns 2.50% p.a. fixed semi-annual interest in addition to gold price appreciation. Capital gains tax exempt on maturity (8 years).
- **National Pension System (NPS)**:
  - Cost-effective retirement vehicle with choice of equity, corporate debt, and government securities.
  - Exclusive additional tax deduction up to ₹50,000 under Section 80CCD(1B), beyond the Section 80C limit.

## Tax Planning Essentials (Sections 80C, 80D, 24b)
- **Section 80C**: Up to ₹1,50,000 deduction for EPF, PPF, ELSS mutual funds, Life Insurance premiums, 5-year Tax-Saver FDs, and home loan principal repayment.
- **Section 80D**: Up to ₹25,000 deduction for self/family health insurance; additional ₹50,000 for parents aged 60+.
- **Section 24(b)**: Up to ₹2,00,000 deduction on home loan interest for self-occupied residential property.
