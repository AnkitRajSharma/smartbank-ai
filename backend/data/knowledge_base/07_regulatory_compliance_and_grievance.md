# Regulatory Compliance, KYC Norms, and Grievance Redressal

## Know Your Customer (KYC) Guidelines
- Mandatory regulatory requirement under RBI Master Directions and the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA).
- **Officially Valid Documents (OVD)**:
  - Passport
  - Driving Licence
  - Proof of possession of Aadhaar number (with first 8 digits masked)
  - Voter's Identity Card issued by the Election Commission of India
  - Job card issued by NREGA duly signed by an officer of the State Government
  - Letter issued by the National Population Register containing details of name and address
- **Mandatory Tax Identifier**: PAN (Permanent Account Number) or Form 60/61 is mandatory for opening bank accounts, term deposits above ₹50,000, and loan applications.
- **Periodic Updation (Re-KYC)**:
  - High-risk customers: Every 2 years.
  - Medium-risk customers: Every 8 years.
  - Low-risk customers: Every 10 years.
  - Video KYC (V-KYC) enabled for seamless digital completion without branch visits.

## Customer Grievance Redressal Mechanism
- **Three-Tier Escalation Matrix**:
  - **Level 1 (Branch / Online Support)**: Contact the Branch Manager, register complaint via SmartBank AI mobile app, or email support@smartbank.ai. Expected resolution: 7 business days.
  - **Level 2 (Principal Nodal Officer - PNO)**: If unsatisfied with Level 1 or unaddressed within 7 days, escalate to the Principal Nodal Officer at pno@smartbank.ai. Expected resolution: 10 business days.
  - **Level 3 (Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme)**: If the grievance is not resolved within 30 days or the resolution is rejected, the customer can file an appeal with the RBI Ombudsman at https://cms.rbi.org.in or via toll-free helpline 14448.

## Fair Practices Code & Lending Transparency
- **Key Fact Statement (KFS)**: Before sanctioning any retail loan, SmartBank provides a standardized Key Fact Statement detailing:
  - Annual Percentage Rate (APR) including all processing fees and incidental charges.
  - Recovery mechanism and penal charges (which must be reasonable and cannot be compounded).
  - Cooling-off / look-up period of 3 business days for retail personal loans allowing borrower to exit without prepayment penalties.
